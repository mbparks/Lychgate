STATION 10

About

A local-first supernatural field practice.

LYCHGATE v5.3.0

LYCHGATE turns walking, local survey geometry, deterministic world generation, generated field tradition, Relics, Ley lines, persistent Shrouds, planned Expeditions, local replay records, portable Region Packages, Surveyor authoring, regional Lore Cabinets, finite authored Scenarios, and asynchronous Passage Stone artifacts into a private field game. The Kindled and the Sextons disagree about mercy. Neither order is written as the villain. Generated lore and encounter text are game fiction and are not historical evidence about real places.

Privacy

Location data never leaves this device unless you explicitly place location-bearing data into an artifact you choose to exchange. LYCHGATE has no required server, account, analytics, tracking pixel, tile service, or remote font. Cryptographic identity keys, Expedition tracks, replay records, Registry trust, and relay logs are generated and stored locally. Optional relay transports move only artifacts the player chooses to send. Region Packages are exported only when the player chooses to create one.

Asset budget

Bundled art remains hand-authored SVG and CSS. Audio is synthesized at runtime with WebAudio. User-added field photographs are save data, not shipped assets.

Vendored code

The local QR renderer uses QRCode for JavaScript by Kazuhiko Arase, with modifications distributed through qrcode-terminal. License notices are included in the vendor folder. No vendored code makes network requests.

Safety code

  • Stay on public, legal, safely accessible ground.
  • Never enter active rail rights of way, highways, private property, posted land, closed areas, or unsafe terrain for the game.
  • Do not interact while moving faster than the game permits.
  • Treat cemeteries and places of worship as places deserving quiet and respect.
  • Stop play when weather, traffic, visibility, fatigue, or local conditions make continuing unwise.
  • The game never outranks a sign, fence, law, landowner, or common sense.
